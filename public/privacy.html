Privacy Policy

Day Planner · Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version: your plans stay on your device. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your tasks, your calendar or your schedule.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your plan (tasks, blocks, notes, settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • On Android, your plan may be included in your phone's own Google backup if you've turned that on in Android settings. That backup is controlled by Google and your Google account, not by us.
  • Backups you export (.json or .ics files) are saved wherever you choose. We never receive them.

Permissions (Android)

  • Notifications: to remind you when a block starts and to show the optional Now card. Asked only when you turn these on.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time. Optional; the app works without it.
  • Calendar (read-only): to show events from calendars already on your phone on your timeline. Asked only when you tap Show my phone's calendars. Events are read on your device, used only for display and free-time calculations, and never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared.
  • Run at startup: to restore reminders after your phone restarts.

Purchases

Day Planner Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We don't receive or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your subscription is active, and Google's handling of that data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

None. Day Planner contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and doesn't send your data to us or to third parties. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Day Planner isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all tasks, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there's nothing else to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Phacharapol18/Day-planer/issues.
